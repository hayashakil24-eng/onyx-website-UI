Who We Are

ONYKS Solutions is a team of professional legal consultants dedicated to empowering businesses across Pakistan. We specialize in corporate and legal services tailored to the unique needs of entrepreneurs, startups, and established companies.

Our mission is to simplify the complexities of business formation, compliance, and branding so that you can focus on growth. Whether you're launching a new venture or expanding an existing one, ONYKS is your trusted partner every step of the way.

We assist with company incorporation (Private Limited, SMC, NGOs, Foreign Entities), SECP registration, BOI approvals, and legal documentation including memorandums, articles, and board resolutions.

Our team also provides branding services such as logo, stationery, and website design, along with drafting internal rules and operating agreements.

We help businesses register for NTN, GST, and meet FBR compliance requirements, including annual report filing and regulatory documentation.
