Taxation in Pakistan

Tax Rates on Sole Proprietorships
  • NTN issued in owner name
  • Taxed on profits only
  • Return filing mandatory
  • Deadline: 30 September
Tax Rates on AOP / Partnerships
  • Registered: partners not taxed separately
  • Unregistered: partners file individually
  • Deadline: 30 September
Private Limited Companies
  • Small Company: 24%
  • Other than Small: 29%
  • Minimum Turnover Tax: 1.25%
