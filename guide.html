Frequently Asked Questions

Find professional answers to your legal and business queries.

I want to start a business but don't know the structure. Can you explain?

There are basically the following types of business structures in Pakistan: Sole Proprietorships, Partnerships, Limited Liability Partnerships (LLP) and Limited Companies. Each differs in taxes, liability, and control.

What is a Sole Proprietorship & how to register?

A business run by an individual. No separate legal entity; the owner takes all profits/losses and has unlimited liability. Taxes are paid via personal returns.

Registration Steps:
  1. Choose a name and make a letterhead/business card.
  2. Take CNIC and letterhead to a bank for a business account.
  3. Apply for NTN/GST at FBR (Requires rent agreement & utility bill).

What is a Partnership (Firm / Form C)?

Works like a sole proprietorship but with multiple individuals. Requires a partnership agreement on legal stamp paper. Partners have unlimited liability, meaning if one is sued, all are liable.

What is a Company (Private/Public Limited)?

A separate legal entity owned by shareholders. Offers limited liability protection. Requires more paperwork (Board of Directors, Auditor, Legal Advisor) but is highly transparent.

What is EOBI & Social Security? Are they same?

EOBI: Pension/Old-age benefits. Mandatory for 5+ employees (5% employer, 1% employee contribution).

Social Security: Health/Workplace insurance (6% employer contribution). Both are mandatory under Pakistani law.

Why should I apply for a Trademark or Copyright?

To prevent others from stealing your brand identity or creative work. It acts as legal notice of your ownership and allows you to sue for misuse.
